## Slide 2: The Problem
Most people don't know where their money goes. 
- Bank statements are cryptic (e.g. `TST* SBUX 4921`).
- Manual budgeting apps are tedious.
- **Result:** We earn, spend, and end the month surprised at how little remains.

---

## Slide 3: The Koshin Solution
An automated web application that turns raw transaction data into real financial understanding.
- **Instant Insights:** Upload a CSV and Koshin instantly categorizes and visualizes your spending.
- **Financial Advisor:** Not just a tracker, but an active advisor offering plain-language guidance.

---

## Slide 4: Hybrid Categorization Engine
How we clean your data:
1. **Deterministic Pattern Matching:** (Confidence: 0.95+) For major brands.
2. **N-Gram Vector Similarity:** (Confidence: 0.80+) For character clusters.
3. **Levenshtein Edit Distance:** (Confidence: 0.70+) Typo-tolerant normalization.

---

## Slide 5: The Financial Health Score
A dynamic 0-100 score based on your true financial reality.
- **Savings Velocity**
- **Net Liquidity Reserve**
- **Subscription-to-Income Ratio**
*Koshin analyzes these to grade your monthly health.*

## Slide 6: Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP 3
- **Backend:** Next.js API Routes, NextAuth.js
- **Database:** Prisma ORM, SQLite (LibSQL)
- **Utilities:** PapaParse, Lucide Icons
